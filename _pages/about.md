Hi, my name is **Siyuan Gao (高思远)**. My first name is pronounced pretty much as _See Young_, but it's still hard to remember, so you are also welcome to call me **Ricky**.

I am a research engineer evolving with recommenders: **graph ML for people recommendation** (LinkedIn, TikTok) → **long-sequence modeling & pretraining** (Pinterest) → **LLM-native agentic recommendation, RL post-training, and RAG** (Meta).

I was born in Shandong Province, China, and left my hometown for college in Hangzhou, China, where I finished my bachelor's degree in Math and Applied Math at [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/) (2012–2016). I received my PhD in Engineering and Applied Science from [Yale University](https://www.yale.edu/) in 2021, where I worked on **applied machine learning**, **manifold learning**, **network science**, and **neuroimaging** with [Professor Dustin Scheinost](https://medicine.yale.edu/profile/dustin-scheinost/), [Professor Gal Mishne](http://mishne.ucsd.edu/), and [Professor Todd Constable](https://medicine.yale.edu/profile/todd-constable/).

Besides looking at my research, please also kindly check out my [online gallery](https://500px.com/carricky) where all my awkward photographs are posted.
