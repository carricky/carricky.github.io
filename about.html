Biography

Hi, my name is Siyuan Gao (高思远). My first name is pronounced pretty much as See Young, but it's still hard to remember, so you are also welcome to call me Ricky.

I was born in Shandong Province, China, and left my hometown for college in Hangzhou, China, where I finished my bachelor degree in Zhejiang University. I received my Ph.D. from Yale University in 2021. I am currently a Research Engineer at Meta, working on LLM-native agentic recommendation, RL post-training, and RAG. Before Meta, I worked on recommender systems at Pinterest (long-sequence modeling and pretraining for ads ranking), TikTok (social recommendation), and LinkedIn (People You May Know). Besides looking at my research, please also kindly check out my online gallery where all my awkward photographs are posted.
